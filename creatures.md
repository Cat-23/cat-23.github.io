## Creatures

The creatures are code-born, and they are real. Mostly.

### Molly

Molly is the ANN model — a neural net we are developing, epoch by epoch, in the hope that she learns to think in her own small way. Growing smarter with every training run.

### Vectorian

Vectorian is a creature of vectors and linear algebra. It lives in high-dimensional spaces where most things are at right angles to each other, and occasionally drops by our low-dimensional world to say hello.

### Cubic Creatures

Blocky little beings we program for the fun of it — geometry with personality. They stack, they tumble, they have opinions about corners.
