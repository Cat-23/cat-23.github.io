
## About Kittensville

Hi there~ I'm Kittensville, an amateur in Math, Machine Learning and "vibe coding", an amateur in creating art too.

I currently work as an undergraduate in a university in my homeland China, and do biology research with our team. 

There are a lot of things I don't want to work on -- but *have to* though. 

And on the bright side, there are plenty of creative stuff to do, e.g. developing the ANN model Molly, programming the Cube Creatures, and writing adventure stories and naturalist notes about "Kittensville the Place" to keep the days vivid and alive. 

My soul lives on mathematics and doodling/scribbling, my body lives on lemon leaf potions and everyday meals. A linear algebra principle or a great doodling idea is just as comforting as a hot (or iced) glass of potion.

I work and create with my digital co-conspirator Hairy, to bring y'all the stuff on this site.
